# Football naming sources and limits

Checked: 2026-10-10. `src/data/competitionClubs.ts` contains a name-only public snapshot of 18 Portugal, 20 Brazil, 20 Italy and 22 Spain second-tier clubs for the noted seasons; this is a manual layer, not a complete football database or live feed. The pathway picker offers a three-club sample per nationality. Club level, role, expected minutes and offer scenario are game-created estimates; they are not factual club/player statistics, recruitment decisions or real offers. The current competition simulation remains separate and keeps generated/sample player records. There are no real squad, player, staff, photo or badge records in this layer.

## Rights and update policy

- These citations establish public factual references, **not redistribution, endorsement, trademark or game-use permission**. No licence has been confirmed. Do not describe any source's data as licensed.
- Names alone are used in the pathway-choice interface. Do not add badges, photographs, copied rosters, staff lists or proprietary player statistics without an independently confirmed legal basis.
- The in-game roles, level ratings, expected minutes and simulated offer framing are invented approximations. The pathway choice is metadata only; it does not add a real club or its real squad to the match engine, and does not mean the named club made an offer.
- Brazil is a calendar-year 2026 source, not a 2026/27 league season. Portugal's official source currently verifies season dates but not club list; the picker labels the team-list source as secondary. All data must be refreshed and re-verified before being advertised as current or complete.
